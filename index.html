About BloodConnect

Technology that connects people when it matters.

BloodConnect is a blood donation network designed to simplify the process of finding donors and managing blood requests.

The platform provides donor registration, availability management, blood-group search, verified requests, donor responses, donation history and an administrative dashboard.
